Program description

When you call a participating business and your call is not answered, you receive a text message from that business's number so you can continue the conversation by text. Business owners also receive text alerts about missed calls.

How you opt in

You opt in by calling or texting a participating business's phone number. Only callers whose call goes unanswered receive a text reply. We do not send marketing messages.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. You typically receive one text after a missed call, plus replies in any conversation you continue.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will receive one confirmation and no further texts. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at callwranglertx@gmail.com.
