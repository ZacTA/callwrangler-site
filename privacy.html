Privacy Policy

Call Wrangler, operated by Zachary Altman, a sole proprietor in Liberty Hill, Texas. Last updated: October 8, 2026.

What this service does

Call Wrangler sends an automated text message to people whose phone call to a participating business was not answered, so they can continue the conversation by text. It also sends alerts about missed calls to the business owner.

Information we collect

  • The phone number of the person who called or texted.
  • The date and time of the call or message.
  • The content of text messages exchanged with the business's number.

We do not collect names, addresses, or payment information from callers.

How we use it

We use this information only to reply to the caller, to notify the business they called, and to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We share information only with the business the person contacted and with service providers that deliver our messages and host our systems (such as our telecommunications provider), who may use it only to provide those services.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help. After you reply STOP, you will receive one confirmation message and no further texts.

Retention and security

We keep call and message records only as long as needed to provide the service to the participating business, and we protect them with reasonable technical safeguards.
