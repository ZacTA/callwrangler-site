Call Wrangler

Missed-call text-back for local businesses. Operated by Zachary Altman, Liberty Hill, Texas.

What we do

When someone calls a business we serve and nobody can answer, Call Wrangler texts the caller right away so they can keep the conversation going by text. The business owner gets an alert with the caller's number.

How text messages work (SMS opt-in)

Call us at (737) 252-3492. By calling or texting this number, you agree to receive a text reply about your call if we miss it.

  • You only receive texts after you contact us first. We never send marketing messages.
  • Message frequency varies; usually one text per missed call, plus replies in any conversation you continue.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help.
